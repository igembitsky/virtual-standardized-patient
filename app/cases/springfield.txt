[ANSWER]
most likely: Menopause or perimenopause
also accepted: hyperthyroidism, malignancy, anxiety attack, pregnancy, infection, pheochromocytoma, alcohol abuse, dietary triggers such as spicy food
tests: TSH or thyroid function tests, FSH level, pregnancy test, LH level, estrogen level, cancer labs such as CA-125
pass diagnosis words: menopaus*, perimenopaus*, peri-menopaus*, climacteric, change of life, hypoestrogen*, vasomotor
pass test words: tsh, thyroid stimulating, fsh, follicle stimulating, oestradiol, estradiol, pregnancy test, hcg
teaching point: She is 51, has had no period for six or seven months, and has hot flashes that start in the chest and spread, vaginal dryness, irritability, and broken sleep. Her mother went through menopause at 50. That is menopause, and it is a clinical diagnosis. Check TSH and a pregnancy test to rule out a thyroid problem and pregnancy. FSH supports it but does not make it. Then treat: lifestyle changes such as dressing in layers, lowering the room temperature, weight loss and avoiding triggers, an SSRI or SNRI if she wants a non hormonal drug, or hormone replacement with estrogen and progesterone because her uterus is intact, with a discussion of risks and benefits, and vaginal estrogen for the dryness. Say out loud that her symptoms are severe enough to treat.
miss note: The missed periods and the vaginal dryness are the two facts worth having, and she offers neither. Hot flashes on their own could be a thyroid problem or anxiety. Six or seven months without a period at 51, with dryness and irritability, is menopause.
